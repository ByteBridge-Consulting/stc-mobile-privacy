Delete Your Account

Intercity STC mobile app · Last updated 11 August 2026

This page explains how to delete your Intercity STC account, what happens to your information when you do, and what we are required to keep.

The app is operated by Intercity STC Coaches Ltd. ("STC", "we", "us"). You can delete your account yourself inside the app, or ask us to delete it for you. Both routes are described below.

1. Delete your account in the app

This is the fastest route and takes effect immediately.

  1. Open the Intercity STC app and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the page and tap Delete account.
  4. Read the confirmation message, then tap Delete account again to confirm.

Your account is closed straight away and you are signed out. Everything the app had saved on your device — your sign-in token, your cached profile, your saved emergency contact and your stored bookings — is removed from the phone at the same time.

2. Ask us to delete your account

If you have already uninstalled the app, cannot sign in, or would simply prefer we do it, email us and we will delete the account for you.

Email: PLACEHOLDER@stcgh.com
Subject: Account deletion request

Please include the mobile phone number registered to the account and the full name on it, so we can find the right record.

Because a deletion cannot be undone, we verify that the request genuinely comes from the account holder before we act on it. We do this by contacting the registered mobile number. If we cannot verify ownership, we will not delete the account.

We acknowledge requests within 2 business days and complete verified deletions within 30 days, as required by Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843). In practice most are done well inside that window.

3. What is deleted and what is kept

Deleting your account removes your ability to sign in and removes the personal profile we hold about you. Some transaction records must be kept: Ghanaian tax, accounting, safety and transport rules require us to retain records of journeys sold, and we cannot delete those on request.

DataWhat happens
Your account and sign-in access Deleted. The account is closed and can no longer be used to sign in.
Name, email address, date of birth Deleted.
Next of kin / emergency contact details Deleted.
Data saved on your phone (sign-in token, cached profile, saved bookings) Deleted from the device as part of the same action.
Mobile Money number and PIN We never store your PIN at any point. Payment details are held by our payment partner and your mobile money provider under their own retention rules.
Booking, ticket and payment records Retained for the period required by Ghanaian tax, accounting, safety and transport regulations, then deleted or anonymised. These records are kept for legal compliance and are no longer linked to an active account.

This mirrors section 5 of our Privacy Policy, which explains our retention rules in full.

4. Before you delete

Deleting your account cannot be undone. We cannot restore a deleted account, its booking history or its tickets.

  • Travelling soon? Deleting your account does not cancel a booked trip and does not issue a refund, but you will lose access to your ticket in the app. If you have an upcoming journey, contact us first so we can help with the ticket or a refund before the account is closed.
  • Payment in progress? Wait until any pending Mobile Money payment has completed or expired before deleting, so the booking is not left in limbo.
  • Just want to sign out? If you only want to leave the app on this phone, use Log out instead — it signs you out and clears the device without closing your account.
