Privacy Policy

Intercity STC mobile app · Last updated 11 August 2026

This policy explains what personal information the Intercity STC mobile app collects, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the rights you have over it.

The app is operated by Intercity STC Coaches Ltd. ("STC"), which is the data controller responsible for your personal information under Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

The app is developed and supported by Byte Bridges Solutions on STC's behalf. Byte Bridges Solutions handles account, privacy and support enquiries for the app, so correspondence about your data comes to them rather than to an STC address — the details are in section 10.

1. Information we collect

We only collect information you give us in order to book and manage travel. We do not buy personal data from third parties, and we do not collect anything in the background.

InformationWhy we need it
Mobile phone number To create your account and sign you in. We send a one-time code (OTP) to this number to verify it belongs to you.
First and last name To issue tickets in the correct passenger name and to identify passengers at boarding.
Email address To send booking confirmations and service messages about your trip.
Date of birth and gender To apply correct fares and seating where these depend on passenger category.
Next of kin and emergency contact (name and phone number) So we can reach someone on your behalf in the event of an incident during travel.
Mobile Money number To process payment for your booking. See section 3.
Booking and travel history To show your tickets, support refunds and rebooking, and handle enquiries.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your location. The app requests no location permission.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, photos, microphone or files.
  • We do not use advertising networks, analytics SDKs, or third-party tracking tools.
  • We do not build advertising profiles or sell your personal information to anyone.

The only permission the app requests on Android is internet access (android.permission.INTERNET), which it needs to communicate with our booking system.

2. Information stored on your device

To keep you signed in, the app stores a sign-in token on your own device using the operating system's encrypted storage (Keychain on iOS, Keystore-backed storage on Android). It also stores a small flag recording whether you have completed the introductory screens.

This information stays on your device. Signing out or uninstalling the app removes it.

3. Payments

Payments are made by Mobile Money. Your Mobile Money number is passed to our payment processing partner and your mobile money provider solely to complete the transaction you have authorised.

We do not store your Mobile Money PIN at any point. STC has no access to it — you enter it only on your own network provider's prompt.

4. Who we share information with

We share personal information only where it is necessary to run the service, and only to the extent required. We do not sell it, and we do not share it for advertising.

  • Payment providers and mobile money operators — to process the payment for your booking.
  • Our technology providers — the app uses Amazon CloudFront to deliver images and content, and an external QR code service to render the scannable code on your ticket. The QR service receives only the ticket reference encoded in the code, not your name or contact details.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — where we are required to do so by Ghanaian law, a court order, or a lawful request from a competent authority.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account details for as long as your account is open. Booking and payment records are kept for as long as we are required to retain them for tax, accounting, safety and regulatory purposes, after which they are deleted or anonymised.

If you close your account, we delete or anonymise information we no longer have a lawful reason to keep.

You can close your account at any time, either from the Profile page in the app or by asking us to do it. See Delete Your Account for the steps, and for exactly what is deleted and what we are required to retain.

6. How we protect it

  • All communication between the app and our systems is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • Sign-in tokens are held in the device's encrypted secure storage rather than in ordinary app storage.
  • Access to passenger data within STC is limited to staff who need it to do their job.

No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable steps appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we hold.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) you have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you, and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate or incomplete information corrected;
  • ask us to delete information we no longer have a lawful reason to keep;
  • object to how we are using your information;
  • withdraw consent where we relied on your consent to process it; and
  • complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana if you believe we have handled your information unlawfully.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in section 10. We will respond within the time required by law. To delete your account specifically, see Delete Your Account.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults booking travel. Children travel as passengers on a booking made by an adult, who is responsible for providing their details. We do not knowingly create accounts for children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and change the "last updated" date at the top. Where a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
